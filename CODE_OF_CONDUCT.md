# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. By participating, you agree to uphold it.

## In short

We want Ignitic to be a welcoming community for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

**Expected:** empathy and kindness, respect for differing opinions, giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, owning mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the community.

**Not acceptable:** sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insults or personal and political attacks, public or private harassment, publishing others' private information without permission, and any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all community spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat) and when you officially represent the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to **igniticai@gmail.com**. All reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.

Maintainers follow the Contributor Covenant [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines): correction, warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban, depending on severity and history.
