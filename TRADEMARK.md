## Forks

Forks are welcome. If your fork **diverges substantially** from upstream (a different feature set, a different target audience, or it is distributed as its own product), you must **rebrand it**:

1. Choose a name that is not confusingly similar to "Ignitic".
2. Replace the logos and product name in user-facing surfaces.
3. You may, and are encouraged to, credit the origin: *"Based on Ignitic."*

Removing the Ignitic branding from user-facing surfaces requires a commercial license, unless you are rebranding a substantially diverged fork as described above.
